# NextEra Energy × Dominion Energy | M&A Accretion–Dilution Model

**Independent financial modeling case study | October 2026**  
**Built in:** Microsoft Excel

## Overview

This project analyzes the **announced merger between NextEra Energy (NYSE: NEE) and Dominion Energy (NYSE: D)**, announced on **May 18, 2026**. The Excel workbook evaluates the transaction consideration, implied valuation, pro forma ownership, earnings-per-share (EPS) accretion/dilution, and sensitivity to key assumptions.

The merger terms are based on public disclosures; **forecast earnings, financing assumptions, and EPS outcomes are my own illustrative estimates**, not company guidance.

## Transaction snapshot

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Exchange ratio | 0.8138 NEE shares per Dominion share |
| Additional one-time cash payment | $360 million in aggregate |
| Announced post-merger ownership | ~74.5% NEE / ~25.5% Dominion shareholders |
| Model-implied equity consideration | ~$67.18 billion |
| Model-implied enterprise value of Dominion | ~$120.34 billion |

*The modeled valuation figures use the workbook's stated share-count, price, debt, and cash inputs; they are not official transaction valuations.*

## Base-case results

| EPS analysis | 2027E | 2028E | 2029E |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| NextEra standalone EPS | $4.34 | $4.69 | $5.06 |
| Pro forma EPS | $4.41 | $4.74 | $5.10 |
| **EPS accretion** | **1.59%** | **1.10%** | **0.62%** |

The base case assumes **zero run-rate cost synergies**. For comparability, each year's analysis assumes a **full year of combined operations**, rather than projecting a partial year after the expected closing date. These estimates are sensitive to the underlying assumptions.

## What's in the Excel workbook

The workbook includes seven worksheets: `Cover`, `Transaction Summary`, `Inputs`, `Deal`, `AccDil`, `Sensitivity & Conclusion`, and `Notes`.

It contains transaction valuation and sources-and-uses analysis, purchase accounting, a standalone-to-pro-forma EPS bridge, and **2028E sensitivity tables** for the exchange ratio, synergies, and earnings growth. Assumptions, sources, and analytical limitations are documented within the file.
